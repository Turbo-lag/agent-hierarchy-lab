# A4: Independent candidates and selection

You are the selector and final integrator. Spawn exactly three peer agents, each
asked to solve the complete task independently from the same task and starter.
Use fresh contexts where available and do not share one candidate's work with
another. Assign scratch/candidate1/, scratch/candidate2/, scratch/candidate3/.
No nested spawning. Agents may self-check their own candidate.

Wait for all candidates. Compare their actual artifacts against the task and
run suitable checks. Choose one candidate as the base; make evidence-supported
repairs yourself if needed. Record the selected candidate and selection evidence.
Do not merely vote on confidence, and do not add a separate judge agent.
