Experimental method

Same task and clock. Different teams.

Assignment

Each workflow received the same frozen inputs for its task in a fresh workspace. The operator fixed the model at Astra with High reasoning effort and capped every run at 30 minutes.

Evaluation

The operator froze the final submission at completion. Coding and science earned up to 80 points from executable checks and 20 from review. Research used a 100-point rubric grounded in six source notes. Two AI raters assessed manual criteria.

Interpretation

Session records established who delegated to whom. Grading tracked artifact quality and compliance separately. Each task–architecture pair ran once, and the time cap allowed teams to consume different amounts of inference.

Proposed next study

When does delegation earn its cost?

As inference becomes cheaper, can a hierarchy use a larger budget to solve harder problems? The proposed experiment gives each workflow the same team-wide allowance and varies whether the work can be divided into independent parts.
