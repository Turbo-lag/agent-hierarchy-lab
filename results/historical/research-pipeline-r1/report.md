The claim “BiCGStab is always the best choice because it uses less memory than GMRES” is not defensible. BiCGStab avoids explicitly retaining GMRES's growing Krylov basis, but its documentation presents it as a conditional alternative when restart choices yield an unsatisfactory expense/convergence balance [S3]. Short recurrences do not prevent breakdown or poor convergence, and solver selection depends on matrix properties and operation costs as well as storage [S6]. My inference is that the memory advantage motivates a candidate, not a universal winner. It also does not establish a total-memory ranking for every implementation and preconditioner.
